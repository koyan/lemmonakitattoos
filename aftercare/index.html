Tattoo aftercare

How to look after your new handpoke tattoo while it heals.

The first day

Keep the wrap on until you go to bed.

Every day until it heals

  1. The next day, wash it with water and a gentle soap without perfume. Don't scrub the tattoo.
  2. Dry it by dabbing it gently.
  3. Put on moisturiser: Bepanthol, or a gentle moisturiser like coconut oil or shea butter.
  4. Do this twice a day until it's fully healed. After that, keep putting on moisturiser as needed.

If it itches

Do not scratch it. Put moisturiser on instead.

Keep it away from

  • The sun
  • The sea
  • Animals
  • Very hot showers: if the water is too hot, you will feel the tattoo hurting

Questions while it heals?

Send us a message and a photo, and we will help.
