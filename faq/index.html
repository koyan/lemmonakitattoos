Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Will my tattoo hurt?

Probably not. Every body is different, but most people say it feels like light stinging or a cat scratch. Handpoke tends to hurt less than machine tattoos.

A big tattoo will hurt more: most people only start to really feel pain after about four hours of tattooing. It also depends on where on the body it goes.

How long will it take?

It depends on the kind and size of tattoo you are getting. Contact us with your idea for more information.

How much does it cost?

Again, it depends on the style and size of the tattoo. Tattoos start from €40, and we don't ask for a deposit.

How old do I have to be to get a tattoo?

In Greece you have to be over 16 with your parents' consent (a parent has to come with you to the appointment), or over 18.

How do I book?

Contact us on WhatsApp or Instagram. Send us your idea, where on the body you want it and the approximate size in centimetres.

Take a ruler and actually look at how big a centimetre is. Really. Look.

How do I get there?

We are in Stoupa, on the main road that goes from Kardamyli through Stoupa to Agios Nikolaos (the Kalamata–Areopoli road), next to MotoSteki and opposite the bakery.

It's about 9 minutes from Kardamyli and about an hour's drive from Kalamata. We work by appointment only, so message us before you come.
