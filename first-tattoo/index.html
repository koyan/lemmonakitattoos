What happens in a session

  1. We start with a consultation, where we talk about the details of your tattoo.
  2. We apply the stencil, making sure it is exactly what you want your tattoo to be.
  3. The artist sets up the space.
  4. You get your tattoo.
  5. At the end, we take photos, wrap your tattoo and go through the aftercare instructions.
