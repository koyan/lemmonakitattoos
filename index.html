Why handpoke?

Every Lemmonaki tattoo is handpoked (you may also know it as hand poke or stick and poke). There's no machine, just a needle, ink and a steady hand, placing the design into the skin one dot at a time.

  • Gentle on the skin

    The needle goes in one dot at a time, not thousands of times a minute. Many clients find it hurts less, and the skin is usually less red and swollen afterwards.

  • Easy healing

    Because the skin goes through less, handpoked tattoos usually heal quickly and with little fuss, as long as you follow the aftercare.

  • A soft, unique look

    Handpoke gives lines and shading a delicate, organic texture that a machine can't copy. No two pieces come out exactly alike.

  • Time to get it right

    The slower pace leaves room to talk, adjust details and take breaks. You're part of the process, not just watching it.

  • Quiet and calm

    No buzzing machine. The session is quiet and unhurried, which many people find much more relaxing, especially for a first tattoo.

  • Made to last

    Placed at the right depth by a trained artist, a handpoked tattoo is just as permanent as one done by machine.
